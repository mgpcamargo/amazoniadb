# AmazoniaDB brand mark

`amazoniadb-mark.svg` is the official symbol. It translates the canopy-and-
network idea into an asymmetrical *castanheira* (Brazil-nut tree): six
leaf-shaped nodes stand for the six catalog domains, a seed pod anchors the
trunk, and three buttress roots suggest a river delta. Every curved branch
ends beneath a leaf centre; there are no floating dots, clipped edges,
accidental overlaps, triangles, or stock network loops.

`amazoniadb-logo.svg` is the horizontal lock-up for larger uses. The website
uses the mark alongside live text so the header remains crisp, accessible, and
well adapted to every language. `favicon.svg` intentionally reduces the mark
to three canopy leaves and a rooted trunk rather than cramming six nodes into
16 pixels.

Use the supplied SVGs at their natural aspect ratio. Keep clear space around
the mark equal to one outer-node diameter, and do not recolour individual
nodes or redraw the network with additional links.

- Canopy light: `#E8F0C8`
- Growth accent: `#B4C962`
- Wordmark dark: `#13261C`
- Wordmark green: `#4E7C4F`
