# AmazoniaDB brand mark

`amazoniadb-mark.svg` is the official symbol. It turns the canopy-and-network
idea into a deliberately simple vector: seven canopy nodes and one central
node, all on a non-crossing connection graph above a rooted trunk. Every
connector ends beneath a visible node; there are no floating dots, clipped
edges, accidental overlaps, or raster artefacts.

`amazoniadb-logo.svg` is the horizontal lock-up for larger uses. The website
uses the mark alongside live text so the header remains crisp, accessible, and
well adapted to every language.

Use the supplied SVGs at their natural aspect ratio. Keep clear space around
the mark equal to one outer-node diameter, and do not recolour individual
nodes or redraw the network with additional links.

Palette:

- Forest: `#174A35`
- Canopy light: `#ECF4D8`
- Growth accent: `#9BC36B`
- Wordmark dark: `#13261C`
- Wordmark green: `#4E7C4F`
